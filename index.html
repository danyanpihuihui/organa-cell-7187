Why 7187.bitmap?

7187.bitmap is the first Cell running under the Organa model. It is a live operational unit anchored to a stable Bitcoin-native coordinate inside Hood 3—not a conceptual demo and not the whole Organa project.

This beacon is a beginning, not the full launch. It establishes Organa's first public coordinate and opens a verifiable path into the live Cell without reducing the wider project to its first use case.
